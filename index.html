ISUP Upgrade Risk — AS-Counselling Tool

Predicts the probability that the prostatectomy ISUP grade will exceed the biopsy ISUP grade. Designed for biopsy-ISUP-1-2 patients considering active surveillance, with a Polish-derived rule-in operating point validated externally on a US academic cohort.

Derivation: Polish single-surgeon cohort, n = 820 · External validation: Brigham and Women's Hospital, n = 562 · AS-eligible (biopsy ISUP 1-2) subset: n = 357, external AUC 0.771 (95% CI 0.670-0.846) · Operating point 0.65: PPV 85.5%, specificity 96.6%.
